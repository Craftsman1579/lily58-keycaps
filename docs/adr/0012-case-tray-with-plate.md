# 0012 – Case: printed tray with a switch plate

- **Status:** proposed (draft v8, M2 standoffs in both variants; tenting pockets; snap, pocket size to be tuned)
- **Decided by:** owner (tray with plate, M2 screws and standoffs, no OLED, feet, tenting later,
  separate branch), derived (geometry)

## Context

The owner's board is a **PandaKB Lily58 RGB MX**: a Lily58 Pro derivative with hotswap MX sockets,
per-key and underglow RGB (underglow LEDs face down), two optional EC11 encoders in place of two
keys, ProMicro RP2040 (USB-C), TRRS, OLED. PandaKB publishes no PCB files. The original Lily58 Pro
KiCad file (kata0510/Lily58, MIT) is the best available source – and it fits: the 1:1 paper
template matches the owner's PCB exactly.

## Decision

- **Layout data** (`common/lily58_layout.scad`) is generated by `tools/kicad2scad.py` from
  `Pro/PCB/Lily58_Pro.kicad_pcb`: outline, 29 switch positions with rotation, 5 standoff holes
  (4.7 mm), 2 OLED holes, Pro Micro, TRRS, reset. The PCB is reversible; the data describe the right
  half from the top, the left half is the mirror image. Shared by the case and (later) the cap
  renders – the 1.5u thumb is rotated by 300° (30° from vertical), not 90° as in `tools/scene.py`.
- **Tray with plate** (`case/lily58_case.scad`): the tray follows the PCB outline (gap 0.6 mm,
  wall 4 mm, floor 3.6 mm, 3 mm 45° bevel round the bottom edge). Plate top = PCB top + 5 mm.
- **Plate over the wall, snapped in** (owner, v5: the plate sat loose in a rebate and looked
  poor): the plate (1.5 mm, MX) covers the wall top flush with the outside, its top edge with a 0.8 mm
  45° chamfer (owner: must print cleanly – the plate prints top down, a radius on the bed side
  would overhang too flat, a 45° chamfer does not). A tongue under its edge (1.2 mm, with a 1.5 mm bulge near the tip) clicks into a groove
  in the middle of the wall top (1.4 mm neck widening to 1.7 mm, 2 mm deep). The groove runs all
  round, also along the open controller zone, where a later cover can use it. `part="snap_test"`
  prints a 30 mm piece of wall and plate edge to tune the profiles. Plates are right and left
  again (the tongue is on one side) and are printed top down.
- **As flat as possible** (owner's wish, v3): plate top 15.2 mm above the desk (v2: 17.9); 16.8 mm
  since the tenting pockets (v7) need a 3.6 mm floor. Only
  the 5 mm PCB-to-plate-top of MX and the 3.5 mm parts below the PCB are fixed; the rest was
  trimmed (standoffs → bosses −2.3, floor −0.4). The keycaps (DES vs KAT) matter more
  than anything left in the case.
- **M2 hex standoffs, like the Choc variant** (owner, v8: liked the Choc mounting): an M2 standoff
  (female both ends, 2.7 mm across flats, passes the 4.7 mm PCB hole) sits in a hex pocket in each
  tray boss (Ø 6.5 mm, 4.0 mm high: the 3.5 mm parts below + 0.5). MX uses **10 mm** standoffs
  (z 2.6–12.6), ending 0.1 mm below the plate; sleeves under the plate (Ø 5.2 / 3.6 mm, the nearest
  switch housing is 2.9 mm from a hole centre) stand on the PCB, so the top screw clamps plate,
  sleeve and PCB onto the tray boss. M2x4 from below (2.5 mm of thread), M2x4 from the top (3.5 mm);
  both heads (Ø 3.7 × 1 mm, recesses Ø 4.1) flush in 1.1 mm recesses. Metal threads both ways instead of v6's M3x10
  tapping into a plastic boss. Earlier variants are in the history of this file.
- **Assembly:** standoffs into the pockets, M2x4 from below; PCB onto the tray bosses; plate with
  the switches on top (it snaps into the groove, the switches go into the hotswap sockets); M2x4 from
  the top through the plate into the standoffs.
- **No OLED, no cover yet** (owner's choice, v4): the controller zone stays open; the plate ends
  1 mm clear of the neighbouring keycaps. A separate cover follows later; the support rings under
  the two OLED holes already have M2 pilot holes for it.
- **USB and TRRS** get rounded slots through the top wall, 2 mm closer to the
  inner edge than the KiCad positions (owner's test print).
- **Rubber feet:** Ø 8 × 4 mm, recessed 1.4 mm (pockets Ø 8.4 mm, 0.6 mm floor above), sticking
  out 2.6 mm; four per half. Thinner feet (e.g. 8 × 2 mm) would save another 1.6 mm.
- **Tenting** (v7): two folding feet per half (owner's, housing 66 × 20 × 5.5 mm, hinge on a short
  side) sit in pockets in the bottom, long side across the inner edge: one below the controller
  zone against the long back edge, just behind a screw head (y 87.3–107.6), one by the thumb cluster
  (y 12–32), both from x 25.5 to 91.8 – aligned, so the
  unfolded feet lift the half evenly (owner); the thumb cluster and its screw allow no smaller x –
  and clear of the five screw heads. The pockets are 2.9 mm deep, so the housings stick out 2.6 mm like the rubber feet
  and sit flush with them (owner's wish); that needs a 3.6 mm floor (0.7 mm above the pockets).
  The rubber foot by the thumb cluster moved to (12, 42).
- **Switch cutout 13.8 mm** (printed holes come out smaller than drawn). Confirmed by the plate fit
  test (`part="plate_test"`, 13.7–14.0 mm): the cutout with two notches (13.8) fits best (owner).
- **Choc variant** (`switch_type="choc"`, files `*_choc.stl`) for the owner's second Lily58 with
  Choc v1 switches at MX spacing: with the MX plate height the switches hung in the air above the
  PCB. The 1.9 mm plate now lies directly on the PCB (plate_above = plate_t = 1.9), so the tray wall
  ends at the PCB top. No plate bosses; instead **M2 hex standoffs** (owner's, female both ends,
  6 mm, 2.7 mm across flats / 3.1 across corners, so they pass the 4.7 mm PCB holes) sit in hex
  pockets in the tray bosses (z 3.1–9.1, 0.1 mm below the PCB top). An M2x4 from below holds each
  standoff, an M2x4 from the top pulls the plate onto the PCB and the PCB onto the boss; both heads
  (Ø 3.7 × 1 mm, recesses Ø 4.1) sit flush in 1.1 mm recesses. Metal threads both ways instead of the first draft's
  M3x6 tapping 1.3 mm into the plate (owner's idea). Same PCB outline, holes,
  ports and clearance below the PCB assumed as on the MX board – not measured on the Choc board.
- **1:1 paper template** (`template.pdf`/`.svg`, 100 mm scale bar) to lay the real PCB on.
- Work happens on the branch `lily58-case` until the case is verified.

## Measurements (MEASURE n in the code)

1. PCB size and thickness – confirmed with the paper template.
2. Standoff holes – confirmed with the paper template.
3. Encoders – not answered; the 14 mm plate cutout takes an EC11 shaft anyway.
4. USB-C centre 5.4 mm above the PCB top, no overhang.
5. TRRS jack axis 3.7 mm above the PCB centre (2.9 mm above its top). Position along the edge:
   from the KiCad data, the 9.5 mm slot covers both footprint variants.
6. OLED: removed by the owner.
7. Tallest part below the PCB 3.5 mm; area around the five holes is free.
8. No plate in the kit – the printed plate is the plate.
9. No wireless/battery.
10. Reset switch on the top, about 3.5 mm high.
11. (OLED window – obsolete.)
12. Highest point of the Pro Micro: 9.0 mm above the PCB bottom = 7.4 mm above its top.
13. M2 standoffs: 4/6/8/10 mm, 2.7 mm across flats (re-measured); MX uses 10 mm, Choc 6 mm.
17. Folding feet housing: 66 × 20 × 5.5 mm (pockets + 0.3 mm); exact size to confirm.
14. Switch cutout: 13.8 mm – confirmed by the plate fit test.
